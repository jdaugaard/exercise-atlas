# Contributing to Exercise Atlas

You’re welcome to use the [authoring skill](.agents/skills/exercise-atlas-authoring/SKILL.md) to create and share exercises for your own noncommercial projects under the [project licenses](LICENSE).

If you would like an entry, illustration, or code change included in Exercise Atlas, please open an issue describing the idea first. Before merging outside work, Jakob Daugaard and the contributor must sign a separate agreement identifying the contribution. It must give Jakob exclusive commercial rights in the contribution, including the right to adapt and sublicense it commercially, and permission to distribute it under the applicable public project license. The contributor can continue using and sharing their work noncommercially.

Identify any co-authors, third-party materials, AI tools used, or prior grants of rights so that both parties can confirm which rights the contributor can grant. Keep signed agreements and personal contact details out of public issues and pull requests. Opening an issue or submitting a pull request does not itself grant exclusive commercial rights. Maintainers should defer merging until the signed agreement is in place.
